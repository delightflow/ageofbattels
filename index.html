    AGE OF

    ALEA IACTA EST

    Place historical soldiers on two hosts, then watch the dice decide a classical battle. No install. One sitting.

    d20
    How to play
    1. Choose Red or Blue, choose a unit, and click the map. You may deploy anywhere.
    2. Read the blurb. Spears brace against horses, shields blunt arrows, and cavalry punish archers who let them in.
    3. Start the battle. Melee rolls a d8 and shots roll a d20. Critical blows, charges, glances, and fumbles tumble on the die.
    4. A side wins when the other is gone. A skirmish is a few minutes. Even a grand host ends well under half an hour.

    Drag to look around. The view stays where you leave it. Center, or the C key, returns to the fighting.

    How to play

    Sound is synthesized in the browser. Unit names are historical troop types.

    Matchups

    Each cell is the damage multiplier when the row attacks the column. Gold is a strong counter. Pale red is a poor one.
